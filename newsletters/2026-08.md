**BELTRUNNER**

I released a game for GMTKjam 2026! Made in 96 hours. It's a spin (pun intended) on Asteroids, where I layer on a few classic gameplay tricks to change it into a different type of game, more of a survival racer, with a ton more risk and reward and a high skill ceiling. The web version is free to play, and for $5 you can access downloads for macOS, Linux handhelds (Anbernic, etc), Nintendo Wii (sideload), and Sega Dreamcast (sideload; experimental lower frame rate...for now).  
[https://blog.gingerbeardman.com/2026/07/30/beltrunner-game-design-postmortem/](https://blog.gingerbeardman.com/2026/07/30/beltrunner-game-design-postmortem/)

This is the first released game made using Jinks. What is that you say?

**JINKS**

Jinks is my totally new way to make games. It was an idea I had whilst waiting for takeoff to fly back from Japan back in February. I asked myself "how would I like to make games if I wasn't tied to any existing methods" and this is what I came up with. It's a multi-platform engine, innovative IDE, and a language of sorts. I'm slowly revealing more about it as time goes on and as it becomes more solidified in my mind and on disk. Big plans!  
[https://jinks.gg](https://jinks.gg)

**BENQ**

*Not sponsored!* I upgraded from a BenQ RD280UA (pivot arm; 60Hz; great) to a RD280UG (pivot stand; 120Hz; higher contrast; amazing) and I am even happier than I was before. It paid for itself in a month due to my increased productivity. Can recommend whole heartedly. I use it almost exclusively in portrait orientation, with one window in each half of the display, and one long page of code at the press of a button. I've also been using its PIP feature to test BELTRUNNER on Wii and Dreamcast.  
[https://www.benq.eu/en-uk/monitor/programming/rd280ug.html](https://www.benq.eu/en-uk/monitor/programming/rd280ug.html)

And I created a monitor control app because the BenQ one is a real monster:
