I've just released a small app that provides macOS Quick Look and Thumbnails support for AmigaGuide documents. 

- Viewer: open .guide files directly in the app
- Quick Look: preview .guide files without opening them
- Thumbnails: see the first page of the guide on its icon
- Export: save as HTML or EPUB

It's powered by [GuideML](https://www.unsatisfactorysoftware.co.uk/index.php?pg=guideml), with a few improvements and fixes by me.

Download: [github.com/gingerbeardman/AmigaGuide](https://github.com/gingerbeardman/AmigaGuide/releases) (1.24 MB)
