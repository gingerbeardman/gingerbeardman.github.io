Fuji Bar! is a system-tray shell for the PaCifiST Atari ST emulator under Windows 95. Serious use of PaCifiST means editing `.ini` files a lot; this lets you change the usual settings through a GUI and launch the emulator in one click. About 76k of RAM.

Version 1.34. New: Laptop, Parallel and STe directives, plus the Image directive's drive qualifier. Works with PaCifiST 0.47 final. Double-click `.st` / `.msa` images to load them. Multiple TOS ROM images from one folder (`TOS*.*`). Atari ST icon library included.

I wrote this as a third-party companion while PaCifiST was still moving fast.
