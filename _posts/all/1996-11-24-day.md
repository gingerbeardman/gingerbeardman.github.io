Day is a day-by-day alarm and reminder. It sits on the taskbar (or hide it with Ghost) and pops up when you asked it to. Alarms can repeat once, daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly or yearly, with an optional five-minute early warning.

Version 1.33. New: Dawn, a tiny starter you put in Startup instead of Day itself, so you get a yes/no prompt at boot.

Written in Visual Basic for Windows 3.1x. Featured on the TECH Win (ASCII, Japan) cover CD as version 1.32, November 1996–January 1997.
