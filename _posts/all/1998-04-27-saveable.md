SaveAble! adds hot corners to Windows. Shove the mouse into a corner to kick the screen saver on immediately (leaving your desk, sensitive stuff on screen), or to stop it coming on at all (a long compile, a download, anything the saver would only hinder). Plus version includes a system tray icon.

This is the first hot-corners implementation I know of on Windows, years before the OS grew one of its own.

Version 1.38. Display Properties shortcut, new executable. Saver enabled by default on program exit. Windows 3.1x and 95/NT (not Windows 98).

Featured with Quick Paths in PC Answers issue 48, Winter 1997 (Future Publishing, UK), cover CD and magazine.
