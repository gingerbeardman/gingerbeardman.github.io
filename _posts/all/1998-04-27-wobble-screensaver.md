Wobble! shakes the screen until it looks like jelly. Severity from a slight judder to near-earthquake, optional colour invert / screen flip, slow motion, desktop preview, password, and context-sensitive help.

Version 1.40. New: wobble a custom picture, severe slow-motion option, multi-tasking-friendlier, settings dialog tweak. Recompiled with Visual Basic 5 (SP3).

Earlier 1.32 was featured on TECH Win (ASCII, Japan), November 1996–January 1997.

Archived page: [gingerbeardman.com/archive/tektonix/wobble.htm](https://www.gingerbeardman.com/archive/tektonix/wobble.htm)
