Light! is a screen saver that passes a trailing beam across the desktop, illuminating whatever is behind it. You can change the size and speed of the light, and whether it inverts the screen colours.

Version 1.30 is a rewrite: faster, smaller, and with a password. Written in Visual Basic for Windows 3.1x.

Featured on the TECH Win (ASCII, Japan) cover CD, November 1996–January 1997.
