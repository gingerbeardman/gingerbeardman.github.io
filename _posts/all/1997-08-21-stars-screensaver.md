Stars! turns your monitor into a portal to another galaxy. Eight directions of travel, configurable star count and parallax levels, three star shapes, desktop preview, context-sensitive help, and Windows screen saver password support.

Version 1.14. Visual Basic, Windows 95/NT. Uses about 60k. Pairs nicely with SaveAble! if you want hot corners.

**1.14:** password support, three star shapes.
**1.13:** desktop preview no longer stops when you change settings.
**1.12:** always on top of the taskbar.

Featured on TECH Win (ASCII, Japan) and Hot Shareware (Hobby Press, Spain) as version 1.12.
