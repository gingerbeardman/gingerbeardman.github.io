A simple paint program for children, for the Atari ST. One colour for each mouse button. Sixteen-colour ST-low, a two-row palette, fill, shift, line, box, circle, spray, a couple of picture buffers, and load/save of `.MAT` files.

Shareware, developed 1992–94. This is version 1.02 from the nostalgia pack I later put up for PaCifiST. The pack includes a sample picture, `SKY.MAT`: a chunky city skyline and a little plane.

Archived page: [gingerbeardman.com/archive/tektonix/atarist.htm](https://www.gingerbeardman.com/archive/tektonix/atarist.htm)
